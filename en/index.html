Features

Everything you need to turn your samples into a ready-to-play MPC kit.

⤓

Drag & drop your samples

Drop files or a whole folder onto the pads. WAV, MP3, AIFF, FLAC and OGG are converted to WAV automatically.

▦

MPC drum programs (.xpm)

8 banks A–H, 128 pads, multiple kits per expansion. Swap pads, audition by click or keyboard.

▣

MPC expansion with cover art

Add a cover image: it shows in the MPC expansion browser. No image? One is generated for you.

⚙

One Shot or Note On

Choose per pad whether the sound plays in full (One Shot) or only while the pad is held (Note On).

♫

Sample library

Load all your sounds, search, preview, then drag each sample onto the pad you want.

🔒

100% local & private

Your samples never leave your computer: nothing is uploaded. Free, open source, no ads.

FAQ

Making expansions, importing samples, One Shot / Note On…

How do I make a free MPC expansion?

Open MPC Kit Maker, drag your samples onto the pads, add a cover image and click “Download expansion”. Unzip and copy the folder into Expansions at the root of your SD card or USB drive.

How do I import samples into my MPC?

Drag your WAV, MP3, AIFF or FLAC files onto the pads: the app builds a .xpm drum program with the WAV files, ready to load on your MPC or in MPC Software.

How do I add cover art to an MPC expansion?

Drop a JPG or PNG into the cover area. It is cropped square and saved with the expansion, so it shows in Browser › Expansions.

What's the difference between One Shot and Note On?

One Shot: the whole sound plays even if you release the pad (kicks, snares, claps). Note On: the sound stops when you release the pad (long 808s, pads, vocals). Set it per pad with the ⚙ button.

Is it available for Windows?
